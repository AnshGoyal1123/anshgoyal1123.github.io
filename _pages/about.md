Hi! I'm **Ansh Goyal**, a junior studying **Biomedical Engineering** at **Johns Hopkins University**, pursuing the **3+1 BS/MSE program**.

My interests lie at the intersection of **machine learning and healthcare**, and most of my work focuses on building models that help uncover hidden insights in patient data to improve the standard of care.

I conduct research with the [Laboratory for Computational Intensive Care Medicine](https://lcicm.jhmi.edu/people.html) under **Dr. Robert Stevens**, where I’m working on two projects involving **deep learning for neurosurgical applications** (read more on my [projects page](/projects/)).

I’m also currently interning at **Regeneron Pharmaceuticals**, where I’m helping automate internal reporting processes within the **Preclinical Manufacturing and Process Development** department.

I was born and raised in **India**, by parents who were both **PhDs in biochemistry**. We moved to the **United States in 2018**, when I was in **8th grade**, and with the help of some great biology teachers, I slowly traded my fascination with **astronomy** for a love of **biology** (guess it was in my DNA too).  

The real turning point came during an internship at the **Hillman Cancer Center Academy**, where I worked with **Dr. Rafael Ceschin** and his PhD students on a project studying the effects of **congenital heart disease on brain connectivity**. That experience showed me how **data science** could uncover hidden insights into patient health — and planted the seeds for everything I do now.  

From there, I knew I wanted to pursue **biomedical engineering**, and I’ve been fortunate to study it at **Johns Hopkins University**, where I’ve been able to apply that curiosity in all sorts of ways. Through [**LCICM**](https://lcicm.jhmi.edu/people.html), I conduct **neuroscience-focused research**, developing deep learning models for **non-invasive intracranial pressure estimation** and **stroke lesion segmentation**. I also joined a **BME Design Team**, trading my dual-monitor setup for an engineering lab, where we designed a **space-expanding cannula system for biceps tenodesis surgery**. More recently, I’ve been working at **Regeneron Pharmaceuticals**, helping automate internal reporting workflows with a Python-based app that pulls experimental data and generates standardized documentation.  

While I’ve gained plenty of technical experience through these projects, my greatest strength is how quickly I can **learn and adapt**. If I don’t know something (which is often the case), I’m always eager to figure it out — and usually do. I also enjoy **working in and leading teams** and value clear **communication**, skills honed over four lively years as a **high school debater**.  

If you’ve read this far (which I suspect maybe three people have), you know where I’ve been. As for where I’m going — I’m still figuring that part out. What I do know is that I want to keep **solving problems, learning new things, and helping people**. After all, *when you help someone, you help everyone*.
